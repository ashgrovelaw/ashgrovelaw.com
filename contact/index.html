Before you write to us

Please do not send confidential details or documents until we have confirmed that we have no conflict of interest and agreed to represent you. Emailing us does not create an attorney-client relationship.

If you have a court date or a deadline in the next few days, say so in the subject line or tell whoever answers the phone, and we will prioritize it.
